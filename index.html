Independent Retail Research Project

Understanding products, prices, and retail market trends.

NovaCart Market is an independent research project exploring product catalogs, category assortments, prices, and publicly available consumer-interest indicators across the U.S. retail market.

Research Areas

Product Catalogs

Studying product classifications, descriptions, brands, and attributes across retail categories.

Pricing & Availability

Exploring how authorized product listings and availability indicators change over time.

Market Trends

Understanding product assortment patterns and publicly available signals of consumer interest.

Project status: Early-stage research and evaluation. NovaCart Market does not currently operate as a retail seller, and does not claim access to restricted retailer datasets.
NovaCart Market is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Walmart or other retailers. Any use of third-party data is subject to applicable permissions and terms.
